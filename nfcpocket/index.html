Questions

Which tags does it work with?

Any NFC tag your iPhone can read over the standard NDEF protocol — NTAG/Ultralight stickers, ISO 15693 tags, and NDEF-formatted cards. Reading also shows details for contactless cards and fobs.

Can I copy one tag to another?

Yes. Scan a tag, then tap "Clone to another tag" and hold your phone to a blank writable tag to copy the contents across.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on-device. NFC Pocket works fully offline and never sends your data anywhere.

Why does writing sometimes fail?

A tag has to be writable and have enough capacity. Locked or read-only tags, and messages larger than the tag's memory, can't be written — NFC Pocket tells you why.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone with NFC running iOS 17 or later. NFC can't be used in the iOS Simulator, so tag features work on a physical iPhone.
