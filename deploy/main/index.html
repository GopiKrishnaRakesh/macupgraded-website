NFC Pocket

iPhone iOS 17+ Launching Soon in App Store

Philosophy

How every app is built

Software shouldn't spy on you, trap you into subscriptions, or fail because a remote server went down.

01

Local-first computing

Your invoices, files, scanned documents, and NFC keys stay strictly on your physical machine. Where sync exists, it goes through your own private iCloud—never remote servers.

02

Zero accounts & tracking

No sign-up forms, no analytics beacons, and no ad SDKs. The software operates without needing to know your identity or monitor how you use it.

03

Native Swift craftsmanship

Built using Apple's official system frameworks and SwiftUI. Applications launch instantaneously, respect battery life, support system shortcuts, and function fully offline.

04

Dedicated product pages

Each application has its own dedicated page with full documentation, feature guides, and direct download packages (DMG files or App Store links).
